# AniWars

An anime idle tap battler in blocky 3D. Tap to strike, summon heroes from ten banners, beat bosses and awaken your squad.

**Play:** https://treyyy-dev.github.io/AniWars/

## Install on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap ⋮ → **Install app**
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**

It runs full screen in portrait and works offline after the first visit. Saves stay on your device.

## Files
- `index.html` — the whole game
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, icons — what makes it installable and offline-ready
